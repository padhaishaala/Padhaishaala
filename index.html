Our Origin Story

As 10th grade students, we noticed a common problem — almost every student has doubts, but not everyone feels comfortable asking them.

Even when teachers explain concepts, many students still struggle to fully understand. Some hesitate to ask again, while others feel shy or awkward speaking up in front of the class.

Having experienced this ourselves, we wanted to go beyond textbooks and traditional learning methods.

That’s how Padhaishaala was built — to create a space where students can learn freely, ask without hesitation, and truly understand concepts in a way that works for them.
